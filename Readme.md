## About me

I build the backend layer that products stand on: APIs, event-driven services, payment and finance workflows, and the infrastructure that keeps them observable and boring in production. Most recently I worked on backend and distributed systems at **Motive**, and before that I led the Aftersales squad at **Noon** (Dubai), where my team rebuilt the post-purchase journey across payments, logistics, and returns.

I came up through competitive programming (two ICPC Asia gold medals) and I still like problems where the right data structure or the right queue design is the whole answer. These days I spend a lot of that energy on AI integrations, LLM-backed features, and open-source tooling around FastAPI. I also founded **PCPers**, a competitive programming community in Pakistan.

Currently open to **Senior Backend / Platform roles**, remote or relocation.
