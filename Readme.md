## About me

I build the backend layer that products stand on — APIs, event-driven services, payment and finance workflows, and the infrastructure that keeps them observable and boring in production — and increasingly I lead the teams doing it. I've been a tech lead for cross-functional squads at **noon / Namshi** (Dubai), owned architecture and a team-wide restructuring on the Safety Event team at **Motive**, and I'm currently contracting as a **software engineering pod lead**, owning a pod's throughput, review standards and quality bar.

I came up through competitive programming (two ICPC Asia West Regional gold medals) and I still like problems where the right data structure or the right queue design is the whole answer. These days a lot of that energy goes into applied AI — LLM-backed product features, RAG pipelines, agent evaluation — and open-source tooling around FastAPI. I also founded **PCPers**, a competitive programming community in Pakistan.

Open to **senior backend, platform, and engineering leadership roles** — remote or relocation.
